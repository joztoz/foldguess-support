FoldGuess Support

The party word game for foldable phones
Need help or found a bug? Write to apps@maddog.sk. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I play?

  1. Choose the word language, the round length and a deck.
  2. One player describes the word without saying it, the others guess.
  3. Tap Got It when they guess it or Pass to skip.
  4. When the time is up, check the results and hand the phone to the next player.

Ways to play on a foldable iPhone

  • Open, “The describer” (default): the describer sees the word on the big screen.
  • Open, “The guesser (camera)”: friends read the word on the outer screen, the guesser sees the timer inside.
  • Tent: stand the half-open phone on a table. Tap the right half when they get it, the left half to pass.
  • Closed, or any iPhone: hold the phone on your forehead. Tilt down when you get it, up to pass.

Why does the camera turn on?

On an open foldable iPhone, iOS lets apps show something on the outer screen only while the camera is on. FoldGuess uses the camera only in “The guesser (camera)” mode and only during a round, so your friends can see the word. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent. Covering the camera with a finger is fine. If you prefer not to use the camera, choose “The describer” or play with the phone closed.

The word doesn’t appear on the outer screen

Open the phone fully and allow the camera (Settings → FoldGuess → Camera). If it still doesn’t work, tap Play on One Screen or choose “The describer”.
