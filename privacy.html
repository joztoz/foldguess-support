Privacy Policy

FoldGuess · Last updated: November 8, 2026
In short: FoldGuess does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. Everything happens on your device.

Data we collect

None. FoldGuess has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not connect to the internet.

Camera

On a foldable iPhone, the optional “The guesser (camera)” mode turns on the front camera during a round. iOS requires an active camera to let apps show content on the outer screen of an open phone, and that is the only reason FoldGuess uses it. A small live preview is shown on the screen. No images or video are recorded, saved, analyzed or sent anywhere. You can play without the camera, and you can revoke access at any time in Settings → FoldGuess → Camera.

Motion

In the forehead mode, FoldGuess reads the phone’s tilt on the device to detect “Got It” and “Pass”. This data is not stored.

Settings

Your game settings (language, round length, sounds, play mode) are stored only on your device.

Children

FoldGuess does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: apps@maddog.sk

Česky – stručně

FoldGuess nesbírá, neodesílá ani neprodává žádné osobní údaje a nepřipojuje se k internetu. Kamera se zapíná jen v režimu „Hádající (kamera)“, aby se slovo mohlo ukázat na vnějším displeji. Nic se nenahrává, neukládá ani neodesílá.
